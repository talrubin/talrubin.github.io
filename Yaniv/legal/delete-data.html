Delete my Yaniv data

Yaniv, published by Tal Rubin · Last updated 21 August 2026

This page is how you delete your Yaniv account and everything stored with it, without needing the app installed.

If you still have the app, do it yourself in seconds. Open Yaniv, tap the settings gear, then Account & privacy, then Delete my data. That route is immediate and needs no email and no waiting.

If you no longer have the app

Send an email to talrubin1@gmail.com with the subject Yaniv account deletion request.

Yaniv accounts are anonymous — there is no email address or username attached to them — so to find the right account we need something that identifies it. Include whichever of these you have:

  • Your Yaniv friend code, if you know it or saved it anywhere. This is the most reliable identifier, because your player ID can be derived from it directly.
  • Your in-game display name, plus roughly when you last played.
  • The name of a room you hosted or joined, or the display name of a friend you added in the app.

If you have none of these, say so in the email and we will do what we can — but be aware that an anonymous account with no identifying detail may genuinely be impossible to locate, in which case we will tell you that rather than delete someone else's.

What happens next

  • We will confirm receipt and tell you what was found.
  • The deletion is carried out within 30 days of the request, and normally within a few days.
  • We will email you when it is done.

What gets deleted

Everything the app holds about the account:

  • The anonymous Unity Gaming Services account itself — destroyed, not deactivated.
  • Your cloud-saved trophies, games played, games won, and your settings.
  • Any stored match resume snapshot from a match you hosted.
  • Your friend relationships, blocked players, pending friend requests, and presence.

Data stored on your own device is removed when you uninstall the app. If you still have the app installed, use the in-app route above, which clears the device as well.

There are no prerequisites

Yaniv has no purchases and no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel first. Deletion is free and unconditional.

What we keep

Nothing. There is no backup archive, no fraud-prevention store and no regulatory retention period applied to this app. The one exception is described in the privacy policy: if another player hosted a match you played in, their own resume snapshot may still record that match until it is overwritten or their account is deleted. We keep your deletion request email itself only as long as it takes to act on it and confirm.

Deletion is permanent

Your trophies and game history do not come back, and there is no way to recover the account. If you install Yaniv again afterwards you will start as a brand-new guest.
