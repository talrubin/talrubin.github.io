These terms cover your use of the Yaniv mobile game, published by Tal Rubin. By playing, you agree to them. They are short because the app is: it is a free card game with no purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising.

1. The game

Yaniv is a free implementation of the Israeli draw-and-discard card game of the same name. The rules of the card game are traditional and not owned by anyone; this particular app, its code, its artwork and its text are not.

2. Your account

The app creates an anonymous guest account for you automatically. You do not choose a username or password, and there is nothing to sign up for. What the account holds, and how to delete it, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Because the account is anonymous, it is tied to the device and to the cloud record behind it. If you lose access to it, we generally cannot restore your trophies — there is no email address on file to prove ownership with.

3. Playing with other people

Online rooms put you in contact with other players. When you play online you agree not to:

  • Use a room name that is abusive, hateful, sexual, or that impersonates someone.
  • Harass other players, including through repeated unwanted reactions or invitations.
  • Modify the app, or use a modified client, to cheat, to see cards you are not meant to see, or to take another player's seat.
  • Attempt to disrupt, overload or attack the game's services or another player's session.

What we can and cannot do about it. Matches are hosted by a player, not by a server we operate, and rooms are transient. We do not monitor room names or gameplay in real time, and there is no moderation team standing by. What the app gives you is the ability to block a player — from your friends list, a room, or a match — and to report one by email from the same places, plus the ability to leave a room at any time. A report goes to the developer's inbox and is read by hand; there is no guaranteed response time. If someone is behaving badly, leaving is still the fastest remedy.

4. Availability

Yaniv depends on Unity Gaming Services for sign-in, cloud saves and online rooms. Those services can be unavailable, and matches depend on the connection of the player hosting them. The app is provided as-is: we do not promise it will be available, that a match will not be interrupted, or that progress will never be lost.

Single-player games against the bots work without a network connection.
