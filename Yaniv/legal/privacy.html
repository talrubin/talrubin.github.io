Yaniv — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Yaniv is a card game published by Tal Rubin. This policy describes exactly what the app stores about you, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be checkable rather than comprehensive: every item below corresponds to something specific in the app.

The short version. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically so your trophies survive a reinstall and so other players can see you in a room. It stores your game progress and settings. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and it contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers of any kind.

1. The account

When you first open Yaniv, the app signs you in to Unity Gaming Services (UGS) as an anonymous guest. This happens automatically, before you tap anything, because the app needs an identity before it can save your progress or seat you in an online room.

That account is an opaque player ID generated by Unity. It is not linked to an email address, a phone number, a Google account or any real-world identity, and Yaniv never asks you for one. Your display name is generated by the app; you are not asked to supply one.

2. What is stored on your device

The following are stored locally, using Android's app-private preference storage. They never leave your device except as part of the cloud sync described in section 3.

3. What is stored in the cloud

Stored by Unity's Cloud Save service, under your anonymous player ID:

  • Your trophies, games played, games won, and your four settings switches — the same values as above, mirrored so they survive a reinstall or move to a new device.
  • A match resume snapshot (yaniv_match_resume), written once per round only while you are hosting an online match. It contains the state of the match in progress, which includes every player's cards and the anonymous player IDs of the other people at the table. It is overwritten each round and is not read by anything today.

4. Online play

Online play uses Unity's Lobby and Relay services, and matches are hosted by one of the players rather than by a server we run.

  • Other players in your room can see your display name and how you are doing in the match. They do not receive your anonymous player ID — the app deliberately substitutes a per-match seat token.
  • Room names are typed by whoever creates the room and are visible to anyone who can see that room in the public list. Do not put personal information in a room name.
  • Room membership and the room's game settings exist for as long as the room does, and are deleted when the room closes.
  • Your friend code is a reversible encoding of your anonymous player ID. Anyone you give it to can derive that ID. It is not linked to any personal identity, but treat it as you would a username.

5. Friends

If you use the friends feature, Unity's Friends service stores your friend relationships, any players you have blocked, pending friend requests, and your presence (whether you are online). Adding someone as a friend is visible to them.

6. Notifications

Yaniv can schedule local notifications on your device — a reminder that it is your turn, or that a room is waiting for you. These are scheduled by the app on the device itself. No notification is sent from a server, and no push token is collected.

7. What Yaniv does not collect

Stated explicitly, because it is unusually short:

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location data of any kind, precise or approximate.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No payment information — the app has no purchases.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party trackers or SDKs beyond the Unity Gaming Services described above.

8. Who your data is shared with

Your data is processed by Unity Technologies, which operates the authentication, Cloud Save, Lobby, Relay and Friends services the app is built on. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Beyond Unity and the other players in your room, your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. There is no advertising partner, no data broker and no analytics vendor.

9. Children

Yaniv is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app creates only an anonymous account and collects no personal details, there is very little to collect either way. If you believe a child has provided information to us, use the contact address below and we will delete the associated account.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything listed above at any time.

  • In the app: tap the settings gear, then Account & privacy, then Delete my data.
  • Without the app: use the data deletion request page. This is the route to use if you have already uninstalled.

Deleting removes the local values in section 2, the Cloud Save records in section 3, your friends and blocks from section 5, and the anonymous account itself. It is immediate and cannot be undone: the account is destroyed rather than deactivated, so your trophies do not come back and signing in afterwards creates a brand-new guest.

What can outlive a deletion. If another player was hosting a match you took part in, their own match resume snapshot may still contain a record of that match, including your seat, until it is overwritten by their next match or deleted with their account. We do not retain data for any other reason — there is no backup archive, no fraud-prevention store and no regulatory retention period applied to this app.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the data an app holds about you, or to object to its processing. The deletion routes in section 10 are self-service and immediate. For anything else, write to the address below.
